01Pre-check

Ordered gates, negative gates first. CLEANED only holds leads with a proven event category that survived every negative gate. Event-capable but unproven listings (golf, farms, museums, club halls, lodging) go to HUMAN_REVIEW for a site check. Halls with no website go to HUMAN_REVIEW flagged COLD CALL.
Work HUMAN_REVIEW before stage 02. Sort by review_flag:
  • SITE CHECK: open the site. Keep on a Weddings, Private Events, Banquets, Hall Rentals or Rent Our Space page, including party rooms and small community halls that rent for real money. Golf tournaments, corporate outings, member socials, ice rentals and Jack & Jill-only club rentals do not count.
  • COLD CALL: halls and venues with no website (free builder subdomains like wixsite.com count as none) and no real email (filler@godaddy.com and similar placeholders are dropped). Call to confirm they rent; approved rows go to CLEANED and become cold_call in stage 02.
  • CHECK: typo domain: one letter off another lead's domain, usually a dead duplicate.

Still invisible to the gates, so spot-check CLEANED: a venue run inside a hotel under its own brand (Harmony Event Centre was the Courtyard by Marriott's); members-only clubs; municipal ownership with no tell in the domain, email or phone; and dead domains.

02Merge results

Merges Anymailfinder results (joined on domain, both decision-maker and general inbox schemas). No venue is dropped for a bad email: placeholder addresses are cleared and the row falls through to Anymailfinder, then phone. Email verification happens in Smartlead at import.
Statuses: has_email, amf_found_dm, amf_found, cold_call, no_contact. Only the CLEANED tab is read, so move approved HUMAN_REVIEW rows into CLEANED first. Duplicate emails and duplicate websites go to a DUPLICATES sheet (the row with the best contact is kept; separate venues on one group's subdomains, like two ClubLink courses, stay separate). Rows with .edu/.gov emails or websites go to REMOVED. Facebook-only venues stay in and are worked by phone. Anymailfinder inboxes on .org are accepted when they match the venue's own domain (club and legion halls live on .org).
