01Pre-check

Twelve ordered gates. CLEANED only holds leads with a proven event category. Anything event-capable but unproven — golf, farms, museums, club halls, lodging — goes to HUMAN_REVIEW for a site check instead of being guessed at.
Work HUMAN_REVIEW before stage 02. Open each site and keep it only on an explicit Weddings / Private Events / Banquets / Hall Rentals page. Tournaments, corporate golf outings, member socials and club calendars do not count.

Four things the gates cannot see, so check CLEANED for them by hand: a venue that is really a room inside a hotel (check whether the domain belongs to a hotel); a festival with a venue-sounding name; a city-owned venue whose domain is not in the gate 2 list; and an officiant whose listing is tagged Wedding venue.

02Merge results

Merges Anymailfinder results (joined on domain, both decision-maker and general inbox schemas). No leads are deleted: every CLEANED row reaches the master. Email verification happens in Smartlead at import.
Nothing is deleted: statuses are has_email, amf_found, amf_found_dm, cold_call, no_contact, human_review. Duplicate emails go to a DUPLICATES sheet (first occurrence kept); rows with .edu/.gov/.org emails or websites, facebook/instagram sites, or placeholder emails (filler@godaddy.com etc) go to a REMOVED sheet. Verify emails in Smartlead at import; work cold_call leads by phone.
