Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can we serve alcohol?

Yes. The renter needs to obtain a Special Occasion Permit and provide proof of liability insurance.

Is the hall accessible?

Yes. The hall is wheelchair accessible.

Is there a meeting room?

Yes. The upstairs room is available for business meetings of up to 60 people.

How do I hold a date?

Check your date with our rental assistant, or call 905-898-2333. To book, pay a $200 deposit (or 50% of the fee for weekday rentals). The balance and the $200 damage deposit are due 30 days before your event.
