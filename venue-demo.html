Dallas's best-kept secret

A warm brick loft and a string-lit terrace, ten minutes from downtown.

Most couples who want something with character in Dallas end up driving forty minutes out of the city for it. Ascentis Weddings sits inside it — a restored brick-and-timber loft, a string-lit courtyard and an open terrace on the same property, with the downtown skyline standing behind every photo.

We cap our events at 120 guests on purpose. Smaller lists mean the whole property is yours for the day, your guests are never queued or crowded, and we can pay attention to the details that get lost at a 300-person venue.

Four settings, one property

Pick the room your day should look like.

Every space photographs differently, and all four come with your booking — say your vows on the terrace, drink under the lights in the courtyard, eat and dance in the loft.

01 — Outdoor ceremony

The Skyline Terrace

An open-air deck facing the downtown skyline. Sunset lands behind the buildings around 7:40 in the spring, which is why most of our couples put their ceremony there. Seats 120, or holds 150 standing for cocktail hour.

02 — Cocktail hour

The String-Lit Courtyard

Private, walled and strung end to end with café lights, under a canopy of trees. It's the quiet corner of the property — where cocktail hour lands, and where the first look and the family portraits usually happen even when the ceremony is somewhere else.

03 — Dinner & reception

The Loft

Exposed brick, timber beams and tall windows. Climate-controlled, so it doubles as the rain plan for any outdoor ceremony at no extra charge. Seats 120 at rounds with room left for a head table and a band.

04 — After dark

The Dance Hall

A separate room with its own bar, sound system and lighting rig, so the party can get loud without pushing your grandparents out the door. Dinner stays civilized on one side of the wall; the dance floor runs until midnight on the other.

Designed by photographers

Every angle here was built to be shot.

The property was laid out by working wedding photographers, so the light, the backdrops and the sightlines were decided before the landscaping was. No parking lot in the background of your first kiss, no fluorescent glare over dinner — just places that look the way you'll want to remember them.

Good to know

Questions we get every week.

Still not covered? Ask our concierge in the corner of this page — it answers day and night, and it can check dates for you.

How many guests can you hold? +

120 seated for dinner, 150 standing for a cocktail-style event. We don't book above that — the property is built for intimate days, not ballroom crowds.

What happens if it rains? +

The Loft is your rain plan and it's included at no extra cost. We make the call with you the morning of, and our team can flip an outdoor ceremony indoors in about an hour.

Can we bring our own caterer and bar? +

Yes on the rental packages — any licensed and insured caterer is welcome, and we have a preferred list if you'd rather not shop. Alcohol has to be served by licensed bartenders, ours or yours.

Is there parking? +

Free on-site parking for about 60 cars, plus a covered drop-off at the front gate. Rideshare pickup is right at the entrance, which matters at midnight.

How far out should we book? +

Fall and spring Saturdays go roughly a year ahead. Weekdays and winter dates are often open a few months out, and they're the best value we offer.

What holds a date? +

A signed agreement and 25% down. The balance is split into two payments, with the last one due 30 days before the event.
