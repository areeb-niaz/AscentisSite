The short version

A wedding here is small on purpose. Fifty guests, no more than that. The number is the whole design.

The Maple Grove is a private garden beside Smith Mill Creek, planted and shaped over years into several distinct places to stand and say vows. The property was designed by a wedding photographer, which is why every angle already works before a single flower arrives.

We cap every wedding at fifty guests. Not because the lawn runs out, but because past fifty a wedding stops being a gathering and starts being a production. At fifty you get to talk to everyone who came.

Forest and woodsy, Hindu, fantasy, deliberately off-kilter: the gardens are built to take any of it, and nobody here will talk you out of the wedding you actually want.

Before you ask

The usual questions

Can we go over fifty guests?

No. Fifty is a hard cap on every package, and the Pergola site seats thirty. The property was built around that number, and it's the reason the day feels the way it does.

What happens if it rains?

Asheville sits in a temperate rainforest, so we plan for it rather than hope. Rain here is usually an afternoon shower that passes inside an hour. The reception dinner is tented in the Complete Wedding package, and the pavilion covers the after party.

Where does everyone park?

Not on the property. Parking is a short distance away and we run a shuttle for the whole party, guests included, in both directions. Security is on site for the duration.

Do we have to use your vendors?

No. The Full-Day Rental is deliberately bare so you can bring your own team. We keep a preferred vendor list of caterers, florists, photographers, and cars, and we'll send it on request. On the Complete Wedding we vet the vendors and hold the contracts ourselves.

We're planning from out of state. Does that work?

Most of our couples are. The whole booking can happen remotely, vendors and timeline included, so the first time you stand in the gardens can be the week of the wedding.

What does a marriage license take in North Carolina?

You apply in person at the Buncombe County Register of Deeds on College Street, it costs $60, and it's valid for 60 days anywhere in North Carolina. Both of you need to be there with photo ID and your Social Security cards, and there's an online application to fill in first. There's no waiting period, so couples often handle it the day before.

Can we hold a themed or non-traditional ceremony?

Gladly. Forest and woodsy, Hindu, fantasy, gothic, cosplay: nothing about the gardens argues with any of it, and the Tim Burton Spot exists for exactly this reason. Tell your coordinator what you're imagining and they'll tell you where on the property it works.

What if we have to cancel?

Cancel 180 days or more out and you're refunded in full. Closer in, refunds are partial and depend on how close, so ask us for the specifics before you sign anything.

Is there a curfew?

We're in a residential part of West Asheville, so there are noise limits we have to keep to. Bring us your plans for the band or the DJ early and we'll tell you what works.
